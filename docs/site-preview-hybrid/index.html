Upload Review

Queue an export and let SICFUN review it in the background.

Drop in a PokerStars, Winamax, or GGPoker text export. SICFUN imports the file, identifies the playable hands, accepts the upload quickly, and then returns hand counts, EV-loss review, and opponent reads when the async job completes.

Best Results

The strongest reports come from hands that include the hero hole cards. When the export is missing that information, the review flags the limitation instead of guessing. Large uploads stay on the same HTTP path but run as background jobs so the page stays responsive.

    Scope

    Clear product boundaries keep the review useful.

    Implemented

    Ready for review and self-play

    • Upload hand histories and review ranked EV gaps, opponent reads, and model source.
    • Launch Playing Hall batches with configurable hero mode, villain pool, and learning settings.
    • Inspect hero win-rate, action mix, per-villain chip flow, and output files in the browser.

    Experimental

    Advanced modeling stays clearly labeled

    • DDRE and related provider paths are still experimental.
    • Multi-player engine progress does not mean SICFUN is a validated 9-max solver.
    • Experimental paths are not presented as proven poker edge.

    Out of Scope

    No live-table play client

    • No real-table integration
    • No action execution against a poker client
    • No promise of one-click real-time play

    Workflow

    Two paths, same browser surface.

    Upload exported hands to review the costly spots, or configure a Playing Hall batch to compare hero modes and villain mixes. Both run locally and return structured results.

    Upload review

    Drop in a PokerStars, Winamax, or GGPoker export. Get ranked EV gaps, opponent reads, and model source.

    Playing Hall

    Pick hero mode, villain pool, and batch size. Run self-play and inspect win-rate, action mix, and per-villain chip flow.

    Study the results

    Both workflows return structured output boards — spend review time on what cost the most or what the hall run reveals.

    Fit

    Built for local review and self-play study. Not for live automation.

    Good fit

    • Solo grinders doing post-session review or short self-play experiments.
    • Coaches reviewing hand files with students or comparing hero modes in the hall.
    • Teams that want repeatable local evidence from both review and self-play runs.

    Not for

    • Real-time table assistance or client control.
    • Files that do not expose the hero hole cards.
    • Large-scale simulation — the hall is designed for short comparison batches.
